## The PvP cape

Click the **cape button** next to the minimap to put on the **PvP cape**.

- With the cape on, you can attack **other players wearing the cape**, and they can attack you, **anywhere outside town**.
- Everyone starts without it. If you never put it on, nobody can attack you, same as before.
- Caped players have an orange name with a **PvP** tag over their head. While you wear the cape, the caped players near you show as orange dots on your minimap.
- **Towns are safe.** Nobody fights inside town, cape or not.
- **Party and guild members never fight each other**, and neither do characters on the same account.

## Putting it on and taking it off

- **On:** instantly in town, or anywhere when you're not in the middle of a fight.
- **Off:** instantly in town. Outside town, only after **5 minutes without PvP**, so you can't take it off to escape a fight. Logging out doesn't take it off either.

## Losing a fight

A PvP death costs **no EXP** and **drops nothing**. Get up and try again.

## Bounty Hunters and the Wanted

Nothing changes for them: on-duty Bounty Hunters and Wanted players still fight each other with or without the cape, even inside town, and the job war on the trade roads keeps its own rules.

## Area skills

Area skills now hit **every monster in range**, up to the skill's number of targets, and chain skills hop all the way along. They also hit **the players you're allowed to fight**: caped players when you wear the cape, and the Wanted for an on-duty Bounty Hunter. They never touch anyone else.
