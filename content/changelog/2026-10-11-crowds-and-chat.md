## Crowded towns

When more than **100 players** are around you, you now see the **100 nearest**. The rest appear as soon as they come
closer or others walk away. Monsters and NPCs are always shown.

You always see:

- **your party** and **your guild members** nearby,
- the player you're **attacking**, and anyone **attacking you**,
- anyone you've **fought in the last 15 seconds** (PvP cape fights, Bounty Hunters and the Wanted).

Characters don't flicker at the edge: someone new has to be clearly closer before they replace a player you already see.

## Local chat is local again

Like in the original game, **local chat** now reaches the players **around you** (about as far as you can see),
not everyone on the server. You still hear people nearby who are hidden in a big crowd. **Party, guild, whisper and
stall chat** and **server notices** are unchanged.

## Short disconnects

If your connection drops for a moment (Wi-Fi switch, a hiccup, a refreshed tab), your character now **stays in the
world for 15 seconds** and you pick up right where you were when the game reconnects. The game also notices a dead
connection sooner and reconnects on its own. Logging out still leaves at once.

## Smoother movement

Other characters no longer jump when the server corrects where they stopped. They glide to the right spot instead.
